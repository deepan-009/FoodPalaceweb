About us

A family restaurant that keeps Malabar hours

Food Palace sits on the Kengeri Ring Road, opposite Union Bank near Hoysala Circle. It is a casual room with table service, built for groups and for people eating alone at nine at night. The kitchen is halal, the menu runs from a ₹10 kuboos to a ₹1,050 family bucket, and the doors open at six in the morning, seven days a week.

Where the food comes from

The heart of the menu is Malabar: Thalassery dum biryani sealed and finished slowly, mulakittath and kondattam cooked the way they are cooked on the north Kerala coast, puttu and appam at the morning counter. Around it sits a full North Indian kitchen with a working tandoor, and an Arabian section turning out al faham and shawarma off the vertical grill.

Vegetarians are not an afterthought. There are twenty-six paneer and vegetable gravies, a separate veg starters board, veg biryani, paneer fried rice and a whole juice counter that never touches the meat line.
