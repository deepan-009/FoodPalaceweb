A day at the Palace

6:00 am

Steam rises off the puttu moulds. Appam, idiappam and pathal kappa go out to the early shift with tea.

12:30 pm

Biryani vessels open. Thalassery chicken dum at ₹180, mutton at ₹240, both sealed and finished on slow coals.

7:00 pm

Tandoor at full heat. Kalmi kabab, hariyali tandoori and drums of heaven leave the pass in pairs.

11:30 pm

Last shawarma comes off the spit. Falooda glasses go out to the tables that stayed.
